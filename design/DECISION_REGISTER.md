# Phase 2 decision register and governance

Version 1.0 · November 16, 2026 · Authority: POS-DEC-001  
Product owner and sole engineer: **Surely Win Dilag**

## Current authority

POS-DEC-001 approves and closes the requirements-analysis phase and accepts its nine documents, findings, recommendations and proposed direction. It authorizes detailed design and implementation planning. It explicitly retains a final implementation-readiness decision before full application development. No earlier approval cycle is reopened.

The original [BRD v1.2](../Maan_and_Miggys_Beachtro_POS_BRD_v1.2.md) remains the product baseline, unchanged. Earlier analysis artifacts are retained as historical inputs. This register supersedes their pending-decision statuses, organizational assignments and blanket pre-development tax gates. It does not rewrite their historical record. Every internal approval, engineering review, test execution, acceptance and support responsibility belongs to Surely; no additional staff positions or independent QA sign-off are required. External confirmation is sought only for business facts, professional tax/accounting matters, hardware or account access when applicable.

Status vocabulary:

- **Approved directive:** explicitly settled by the current user directive.
- **Design-selected:** straightforward engineering recommendation selected for this design under the authorized phase; no separate approval cycle required. Not a claim that it was implemented or individually approved by a business/accountant.
- **Owner policy pending:** recommended behavior is fully specified for non-production development/test fixtures; Surely may accept or amend it in the single consolidated review below.
- **External/configuration pending:** actual business facts or professional confirmation needed before applicable production use; synthetic fixtures permit unrelated engineering.

The implementation boundary remains separate from design selection. All recommendations are contained in the design documents and backlog, not running configuration. No real tax profile is activated.
