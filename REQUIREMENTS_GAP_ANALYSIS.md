## Conflicts and interpretation cautions

Section 8 calls the transaction format suggested while section 14 states it directly. Uniqueness is firm; exact format, date basis and post-restore behavior need D-12. Section 15's 'queue immediately after local commit' expresses timing but leaves a durability gap; G-04 recommends an atomic intent, not cloud work inside checkout. Sections 15/19 prohibit deleting local records after upload while section 19 allows backup generation retention; D-13 must distinguish independent age/count cleanup from upload-triggered deletion.

The proposed stack in section 10 is not equivalent to a confirmed business requirement, although SQLite and service boundaries are reinforced in sections 5/10 and Appendix B. PHP, Asia/Manila, 80mm, seven generations and suggested schedules remain unapproved settings. No finding authorizes a substitute platform, cloud database, gateway, or additional initial terminal.
